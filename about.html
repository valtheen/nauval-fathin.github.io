ABOUT ME

A data person.
A builder at heart.

Curiosity connects the dots. Building makes them useful.

I'm Nauval — a data person just as comfortable writing a PySpark job over 7 million rows as building the React front-end that surfaces the result.

My favorite problems live in the gap between "we have data" and "we made a decision." That means EDA that finds the story, models that actually generalize, and dashboards a non-technical stakeholder can read in ten seconds.

I've done KPI reporting for live marketing campaigns, fine-tuned transformers for sentiment at scale, and shipped full-stack platforms used in real university operations. I like work that survives contact with reality.
